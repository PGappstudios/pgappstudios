Baby Growth Tracker Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Baby Growth Tracker Pro has no accounts, no sign-up and no analytics. Everything you enter stays in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. We cannot see it, we cannot recover it, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

There are two exceptions. If you tap "Find pharmacies near me", the app asks Apple Maps for pharmacies around you (section 4). And if you buy Plus, a service called RevenueCat checks your purchase with Apple so the app knows what to unlock (section 6). Neither receives anything you entered about yourself or your baby.

1. WHO WE ARE

Baby Growth Tracker Pro is developed and published by Pedro Gregório, trading as PG App Studios, based in Portugal. Contact: pgmetastudios@gmail.com. Where data protection law requires a controller to be named, that controller is PG App Studios. As explained below, PG App Studios does not receive, store or process any of your personal data.

2. WHAT THE APP STORES ON YOUR DEVICE

The app saves the following on your device so that it works between sessions:

  • Your first name, if you choose to enter one
  • Your baby's name, date of birth and sex
  • Weight, length and head circumference measurements you record, and their dates
  • Feeds, nappies, sleeps, baths, temperatures and skin photos you log
  • Vaccine doses you mark as given, and the vaccine schedule country you select
  • Appointments, medicines and other schedule entries you create, including reminder times
  • Notes you write, and menstrual cycle dates if you use the cycle feature
  • Your answers to the questions asked during onboarding, and app settings such as units and language

Some of this is health information about you and about your child. It is stored in the app's private Application Support folder, which other apps cannot read. Skin photos are written as separate files and are marked so that they are left out of your device backups.

3. WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. Baby Growth Tracker Pro contains no analytics library, no advertising library, no crash reporting service, no attribution SDK and no third-party tracking code of any kind. The app makes no network requests to any server operated by PG App Studios, because no such server exists.

The pharmacy search described below is the only feature that uses the network at all, and it talks to Apple Maps, not to us. Nothing you have entered about yourself or your baby is included in that request.

4. LOCATION, WHILE YOU ARE USING THE APP

Baby Growth Tracker Pro asks for your location only at the moment you tap "Find pharmacies near me". Your device then gives the app an approximate position, used once to ask Apple Maps for pharmacies in that area.

  • Your position is never written to the app's storage, never attached to your baby's record, and never sent to PG App Studios. We have no servers, so there is nowhere for it to go.
  • The lookup is handled by Apple, so Apple receives the search in the same way it would if you searched in the Maps app. Apple's own privacy policy covers that, at apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • The app never asks for background or "always" location, and never tracks you between sessions.
  • If you never tap that button, the app never asks for your location and never uses it.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in iOS Settings, and the rest of the app continues to work exactly as before.

5. OTHER PERMISSIONS

Notifications (optional). If you allow notifications, Baby Growth Tracker Pro schedules reminders for appointments, feeds, medicines and upcoming vaccine doses. These are created and delivered entirely by your device and their content is never sent to a server.

Calendar, write only (optional). If you choose to add an appointment to your calendar, Baby Growth Tracker Pro asks for write-only access. It can add the event you selected and nothing more. It cannot read your existing events.

Photos (optional). If you add a photo of your baby, or a photo to the skin log, the app reads only the image you choose.

The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera roll beyond the photos you pick, Health app data, or your advertising identifier.

6. PURCHASES

Plus is sold through Apple's App Store as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment. We never see your card number, billing address or Apple Account details. Apple's own handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To check which purchases are active, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service (RevenueCat, Inc., United States). When you open the paywall, buy, or restore, the app sends RevenueCat an anonymous, randomly generated identifier created on your device and Apple's purchase receipt. RevenueCat verifies the receipt with Apple and tells the app whether Plus is active. It does not receive your name, email, or anything you entered about yourself or your baby, and we do not use it to track you across other apps. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. The app then stores the yes-or-no answer on your device so Plus keeps working offline.

7. DEVICE BACKUPS

If you have iCloud Backup or encrypted computer backups turned on, your device may include Baby Growth Tracker Pro's stored data in that backup, in the same way it does for other apps. Skin photos are deliberately excluded. That backup is created and controlled by Apple and by you, and we have no access to it.

8. CHILDREN

Baby Growth Tracker Pro is intended for parents, carers and expectant parents aged 18 or over. It is not directed at children. The app holds information about a child, but that information is entered by an adult and never leaves that adult's device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect personal information at all.

9. SHARING AND SELLING

We do not sell, rent, share, trade or disclose your personal data, because we do not hold it. There are no advertising partners and no data brokers. The only third-party processor is RevenueCat, described in section 6, and it processes purchase data only.

10. RETENTION AND DELETION

Because your data lives only on your device, you control how long it is kept. Individual entries can be deleted inside the app, and deleting the app removes all of its stored data permanently. We cannot delete data for you, and we cannot restore it if you delete it. Please be aware there is no cloud copy.

11. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, the UK GDPR, Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, the California Consumer Privacy Act or similar laws, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to and port your personal data.

In Baby Growth Tracker Pro these rights are exercised directly on your device, because that is the only place your data exists. You can view every entry in the app, edit it, and delete it. There is no request to send us, and no account for us to look up. If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may complain to your local supervisory authority; in Portugal that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt).

12. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Nothing you enter leaves your device, so there is no transfer of your records to any other country. The only outbound requests are the pharmacy lookup, which goes to Apple, and purchase verification, which goes to RevenueCat in the United States under its standard contractual clauses. Both carry no data about you or your baby.

13. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes for the version that introduces them.
