How well do you really know basketball? These 40 questions run from warm-up to expert, covering the game's origins, the legends, the records and the rules. Answers are under each section, so cover them up and keep score.

## Round 1: Warm-up

1. How many players does each team have on the court at once?
2. How high is a regulation basketball rim?
3. How long is an NBA game, not counting overtime?
4. How far is the free-throw line from the backboard?
5. How long is the NBA shot clock?
6. Who invented basketball?
7. In what year was basketball invented?
8. What did the first players shoot into?
9. In which US state was the game invented?
10. How many points is a shot from behind the arc worth?

**Answers:** 1. Five. 2. 10 feet (3.05 m). 3. 48 minutes: four 12-minute quarters. 4. 15 feet. 5. 24 seconds. 6. James Naismith. 7. 1891. 8. Peach baskets. 9. Massachusetts (Springfield). 10. Three points.

## FAQ

### What are good basketball trivia questions for beginners?

Start with the basics: players on court (five), rim height (10 feet), game length (48 minutes in the NBA) and who invented the game (James Naismith, 1891).

### Who scored 100 points in an NBA game?

Wilt Chamberlain, for the Philadelphia Warriors against the New York Knicks on 2 March 1962.

### Who has the most NBA championships?

Bill Russell won eleven championships with the Boston Celtics, the most of any player.

### Who is the NBA's all-time leading scorer?

LeBron James, who passed Kareem Abdul-Jabbar's record in February 2023.
