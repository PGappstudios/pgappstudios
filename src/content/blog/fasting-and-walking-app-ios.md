Intermittent fasting and walking work well together: one shapes *when* you eat, the other keeps you moving. **Fasting and Walking** tracks both in one clean app, without accounts or cluttered menus, and explains what is happening in your body as the hours pass.

## The fasting timer

Pick a plan and start the clock. The timer supports the most popular fasting schedules:

- **12 and 14 hours**, a gentle starting point
- **16:8**, the most popular daily schedule
- **18:6 and 20:4**, for experienced fasters
- **OMAD** (one meal a day)
- **24, 48 and 72-hour** and longer fasts

Progress rings show how far into your fast you are at a glance.

## Fasting stages, explained

What actually happens during a fast? The app walks you through each stage as you reach it, in plain language:

1. Early fasting
2. Glycogen depletion
3. Fat burning
4. Ketone production
5. Light ketosis
6. Deep ketosis
7. Autophagy
8. The extended-fasting metabolic stage

Understanding the stages makes the harder hours easier: you know *why* you are waiting, not just how long.

## Steps and hydration

- **Daily step tracking** counts your walking automatically, with its own progress ring.
- **Hydration guidance** helps you keep drinking enough water, which matters during a fast.

Walking during a fasting window is gentle, low-risk movement that supports weight-management goals and helps the time pass.

## How to start safely

1. **Begin with 12 hours.** Most of that is overnight. Move to 14, then 16:8, over a couple of weeks.
2. **Drink water.** Water, black coffee and plain tea are usually fine during a fast; check what works for you.
3. **Walk after meals.** A 10 to 15-minute walk after eating is an easy habit to build.
4. **Break the fast gently.** A normal, balanced meal, not a feast.
5. **Be careful with long fasts.** 24 to 72-hour fasts are not for beginners. Talk to a doctor first.
6. **Stop if you feel unwell.** Dizziness, faintness or feeling very weak mean it's time to eat.

12 and 14 hours, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD, and 24, 48 and 72-hour or longer fasts.

### Does Fasting and Walking count my steps?

Yes. It tracks your daily steps alongside your fasting timer, each with its own progress ring.

### Does it explain what happens during a fast?

Yes. It describes each stage, from glycogen depletion through ketosis and autophagy, in plain language.

### Do I need an account?
