Think you know the beautiful game? From the black-and-white heroes of the past to the superstars lighting up stadiums today, football history is full of moments worth remembering. Below are twenty trivia questions spanning every era. Read through, keep score in your head, and check your answers in the section at the bottom. No peeking until you have committed to a guess.

## Why Football Trivia Is So Much Fun

Football is more than results and league tables. It is a living story stretching back more than a century, full of unforgettable characters, dramatic finals, and moments that fans still argue about decades later. Trivia is one of the best ways to celebrate that history. It sharpens your memory of the game, sparks friendly debate, and connects generations, since a grandparent who watched Pele and a teenager who grew up on Messi can meet somewhere in the middle.

Good trivia also teaches. Every question you get wrong is a small doorway into a story you did not know, a player you never saw, or a tournament that shaped the modern game. That is why quizzing is not just a test but a way to fall deeper in love with football.

## The Rules

Simple. Twenty questions, one point each. Answers are all well-known facts, so a true fan should manage a respectable score. Eighteen or more and you are in legend territory. Ready?

20. Which trophy is awarded annually to the player judged the best in the world, historically presented by France Football?

## Make It a Group Game

Trivia is at its best shared. Read these questions aloud at a family gathering, during half-time of a match, or on a long car journey, and watch how quickly a friendly competition breaks out. Split into teams, keep score, and give a small prize to the winner. You will be surprised how much passion twenty simple questions can generate, and how often the person you least expect turns out to be a hidden football encyclopaedia.

For younger fans, trivia is a wonderful way to learn the history of the game they love. Explaining who Pele was, or why the 1986 Hand of God goal is still talked about, passes the story of football from one generation to the next. The facts stick because they arrive wrapped in a game rather than a lecture.

## Tips for Getting Better

If you want to sharpen your football knowledge, a few simple habits help. Watch classic matches and documentaries, not just current games, so you build a feel for every era. Pay attention to the stories behind the players rather than only their stats, because narratives are far easier to remember than numbers. And most importantly, quiz yourself often. Regular, low-pressure testing is one of the most effective ways to lock knowledge into memory, which is exactly why a daily quiz habit works so well.
