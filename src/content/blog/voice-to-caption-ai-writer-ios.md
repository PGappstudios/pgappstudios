You know what you want to say. Typing it out, finding the right tone for each platform and adding hashtags is what eats twenty minutes. **Voice To Caption** removes that step: hold a button, talk for about ten seconds, and get something finished you can post, send or publish, without touching a keyboard.

## What it writes

Voice To Caption turns what you say into:

- **Social captions** with hashtags and the right voice for each app
- **Messages** that sound like a person, not a template
- **Emails**, with the subject line written for you
- **YouTube titles and descriptions** built to be found
- **Product listings** with a searchable title and clear bullet points

## Speak one language, publish in another

This is where it gets genuinely useful. You can choose from **41 languages**, including European and Brazilian Portuguese as separate options, and your output does not have to be in the language you spoke. Talk in Portuguese and publish in English. Describe a product in Spanish and list it in German.

Publishing in a language you don't read is nerve-wracking, so one tap translates the finished post back into the language you actually spoke, hashtags included. Nothing goes out that you haven't understood.

## How it works

1. **Hold the button and talk.**
2. **Pick where it's going**: Instagram, an email, a listing, a YouTube video.
3. **Copy it, or open the app you're posting to.**

That's the whole thing. Everything you make is saved on your phone automatically, and you can favourite the ones worth reusing.

## Privacy

There is no account and no sign-up. Your recordings are turned into text and then deleted, never stored.

## Tips for better results

1. **Say who it's for.** "For my bakery's Instagram, friendly tone" gets a better caption than just describing the cake.
2. **Give the one detail that matters.** A price, a date, a place, a feeling. Specifics make posts work.
3. **Talk like you'd tell a friend.** Natural speech produces natural writing.
4. **Check before you post.** Use the translate-back button whenever you publish in another language.
