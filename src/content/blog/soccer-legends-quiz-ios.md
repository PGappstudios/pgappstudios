Whether you call it soccer or football, every fan thinks they know the game. **Soccer Legends** puts that to the test with fast, multiple-choice questions on players, clubs, championships and the moments that shaped the beautiful game, from Pelé to the stars of today.

## How it plays

Pick a category, read the question, choose from three answers and race the clock. Each question is like a penalty kick: one chance to score. It is easy to learn and hard to master, which makes it ideal for a quick round on the bus or a longer session at home.

Questions cover:

- European giants and local heroes
- World Cup and Champions League history
- Records, transfers and golden boots
- National teams from every continent

## From Amateur to Legend

You start your career as an **Amateur** and climb through the divisions with every correct answer, just like a real football season. Miss a few and you drop back down, so you need to stay sharp to reach **Legend** status.

Even when you get one wrong, you learn something: surprising stats, forgotten records and the origins of football's greatest stories.

## Coins

Correct answers, streaks and levelling up earn coins. You can spend them on harder question sets and hints when you're stuck. Coin bundles are also available in the shop if you want to unlock things faster, but you can earn them by playing.

No personal data is required and there's no mandatory login. Your progress and coins are stored on your device.

## How to climb faster

1. **Play to your strengths first.** Start with the categories you know best to build coins and confidence.
2. **Learn the World Cup winners.** Tournament history comes up again and again.
3. **Know the big records.** Top scorers, most titles, famous transfer fees. A few dozen facts unlock a lot of questions.
4. **Use hints on hard sets, not easy ones.** Save coins for the questions that really stump you.

## FAQ

### What is Soccer Legends?

A football trivia game for iPhone with quick-fire multiple-choice questions on players, clubs, tournaments and records, where you climb from Amateur to Legend.

### Do I need an account to play?

No. There's no mandatory login, and your progress is stored on your device.

### What are coins used for?

Coins unlock harder question sets and hints. You earn them by playing well, keeping streaks and levelling up, and bundles are available if you want to go faster.

### Does it cover international football?

Yes. Categories include World Cups, national teams from every continent and the major club competitions.
