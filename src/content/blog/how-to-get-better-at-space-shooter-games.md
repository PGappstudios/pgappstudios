Shoot 'em ups (shmups to their fans) look chaotic: hundreds of bullets, explosions everywhere, a boss the size of the screen. Watch a good player, though, and they barely seem to move. That calm is learned. Here are twelve habits that turn panicked dodging into control, on any space shooter.

## 1. Look at the bullets, not your ship

Beginners stare at their own ship. Good players watch the space just ahead of it, where the danger is coming from. Your thumb knows where the ship is. Your eyes should be reading what's about to arrive.

## 2. Make small moves

Big sweeping swipes are the most common way to fly straight into a bullet you didn't see. Most patterns can be dodged with a tiny sidestep. Move only as much as you need, then stop.

## 3. Learn your ship's real size

In many shooters, the part of your ship that actually gets hit is smaller than the ship you see. Test it carefully in an easy level: graze a slow bullet and see what counts. Knowing your true size gives you far more room than you think.

## 4. Stay low and central

Sitting near the bottom of the screen gives you the most time to react to incoming fire. Staying roughly central means you can escape left or right. Hugging a corner leaves you with one way out, and patterns love trapping you there.

## 5. Kill fast to remove danger

Every enemy you leave alive keeps firing. Destroying threats quickly is defence as much as attack. Learn where enemies enter and be there with your firepower ready.

## 6. Prioritise the dangerous ones

Not every enemy matters equally. Shooters that aim at you, or spray wide patterns, should go first. Slow drifters can wait.

## 7. Don't chase pickups into trouble

A power-up is never worth a life. If grabbing it means crossing a stream of bullets, let it go. Another one will come.

## 8. Learn boss patterns one phase at a time

Bosses run through repeating attack phases. Instead of trying to win on the first attempt, focus on surviving one phase. Watch where the safe gaps open, notice the "tell" before each attack, and the boss slowly turns from impossible into a routine.

## 9. Choose upgrades with a plan

Stacking random upgrades rarely beats a focused build. Decide whether you want wide coverage, focused damage or protection, and pick upgrades that support it.

## 10. Use the tools the game gives you

Shields, formations and special weapons exist for the hardest moments. Players often save them "for later" and die with them unused. If a pattern looks unwinnable, that's when to use them.

## 11. Play short sessions, often

Ten focused minutes a day improve your reactions far more than one exhausting two-hour session. Stop when you start making careless mistakes.

## 12. Replay the level that beat you

Your worst level is your best teacher. Run it again with one goal: survive the part that killed you. Then the next part. Patterns that felt random start to feel predictable.

## FAQ

### What is a shmup?

Short for "shoot 'em up": an action game where you pilot a ship and shoot waves of enemies while dodging their fire. Bullet hell games are shmups with especially dense bullet patterns.

### How do you dodge bullets in bullet hell games?

Watch the space ahead of your ship rather than the ship itself, make small movements, and learn the true size of your ship's hit area, which is often smaller than it looks.

### How do I beat a boss I keep losing to?

Focus on surviving one attack phase at a time. Learn the warning sign before each attack and where the safe gaps appear, then build up to the full fight.

### Are space shooter games good on a phone?

Yes, especially games designed for one-thumb touch controls, where you drag to fly and the ship fires automatically, so all your attention goes on dodging.
