*Aquafasty is a habit tracker, not a medical service. Talk to your doctor before starting any diet or fasting plan, especially if you are pregnant, have diabetes or have a history of eating disorders.*

Diets rarely fail because the plan is wrong. They fail because people stop following it, and there is a well-known slump after the first week or so, when the motivation of day one has worn off and results are still slow. **Aquafasty** is built around getting you past that point.

## How it works

You start by answering a few honest questions about what usually breaks your diets. Aquafasty then matches you to one of **seven real, evidence-based plans**:

- Intermittent Fasting
- Mediterranean
- DASH
- Volumetrics
- Whole Food Plant Based
- Alternate Day Fasting
- Prolon FMD

No calorie spreadsheets and no lectures, just a plan you can actually finish.

## Learn your plan, not just track it

Every plan comes with a full guide inside the app: what to eat and what to go easy on, a sample day hour by hour, the mistakes that catch people out, and what the research actually says, with sources named. Guidance follows the WHO, the American Heart Association, the NIH and the NHS.

That matters more than it sounds. People stick to plans they understand.

## Your day in Aquafasty

Each morning you get **five small missions** built from your plan. Clear them and your streak grows. Along the way:

- **Water** is logged in one tap, toward a goal sized to your body.
- **Steps** count themselves while your phone is in your pocket.
- **Fasting** runs on a flexible timer, from 12:12 to 18:6 or your own window.

Small, specific actions beat vague intentions. "Drink two more glasses before lunch" is something you can do; "eat better" isn't.

## Watch it work

## Which plan suits you?

A quick guide to the main options:

| Plan | Best for |
|---|---|
| Intermittent Fasting | People who like simple rules about *when* to eat |
| Mediterranean | Anyone who wants a sustainable, flexible way of eating |
| DASH | People focused on heart health and blood pressure |
| Volumetrics | Big appetites: lots of food for fewer calories |
| Whole Food Plant Based | People who want to eat mostly plants |
| Alternate Day Fasting | Experienced fasters who prefer fewer, bigger decisions |

Not sure? That is exactly what the matching questions are for.

## Tips that make any diet stick

1. **Plan for day nine.** Expect the slump and decide now what you'll do when it comes.
2. **Drink water first.** Thirst is easily confused with hunger, especially during a fasting window. Our guide to [how much water to drink for weight loss](/blog/how-much-water-to-drink-weight-loss) covers the details.
3. **Walk every day.** Movement supports weight loss and mood. See [walking for weight loss](/blog/walking-for-weight-loss-steps-per-day).
4. **Watch the trend, not the scale.** Daily weight jumps around; weekly averages tell the truth.
