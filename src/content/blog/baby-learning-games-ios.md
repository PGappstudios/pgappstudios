Most parents of toddlers have the same two worries about screens: that the app is quietly teaching nothing, and that the screen time never ends. **Baby Games** was rebuilt around both. It is a set of short learning games for babies and toddlers aged 1 to 4, led by Bo, a friendly bear who talks to your child, and every session has a clear beginning and a clear end.

This guide walks through what your child learns, how the games work, and what to look for in any toddler app.

## Meet Bo

Bo is a soft, round bear who guides every game out loud. He asks for things ("Can you find the cow?"), cheers when your child gets it, and helps gently when they don't. Because Bo does the talking, your child never needs to read a word.

Every day Bo picks a **Play Time of 5 to 10 minutes** made for your child's age. When it's done, he hands out a sticker for the sticker book. That small ritual matters: it gives screen time a natural stopping point, which is far easier than taking a phone away mid-game.

## What your child learns

The content is deliberately focused on the things toddlers are actually learning between one and four:

- **18 animals** with their real sounds
- **21 first words**: food, toys, vehicles and things around the house
- **8 colours and 8 shapes**
- **Numbers 1 to 10** and counting
- **The alphabet**, with letter tracing for 3 and 4 year olds

Bo speaks six languages (English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Italian), which is handy for bilingual families or grandparents who speak a different language.

## 12 games made for little hands

Each game is short, forgiving and built around one skill:

1. **Picture cards** that say the name out loud
2. **Find it**: Bo asks, your child taps
3. **Who makes this sound?**
4. **Balloon pop**
5. **Memory pairs**
6. **Shadow matching**
7. **Feed Bo** the food he asks for
8. **Count and tap**
9. **Sort by colour**
10. **Xylophone** with animal friends
11. **Tap-to-colour pages**
12. **Trace letters and numbers**

## Built for toddlers, not shrunk-down kids' games

A good toddler app is designed around what one-to-four-year-olds can and can't do yet. Baby Games uses:

- **Big buttons, no reading, no timers and no losing.** Nothing punishes a slow tap.
- **Gentle help after a wrong answer**, never a buzzer.
- **Hold-to-exit**, so little fingers don't leave a game by accident.
- **Three age levels**: 1 to 2, 2 to 3 and 3 to 4 years, so a one-year-old isn't asked to trace letters.

## For parents

Parents get a simple view of **words learned, minutes played and day streaks**. The parent area and any purchases sit behind a grown-up check, so a curious toddler can't wander into settings.

There are **no ads and no tracking**, and everything works **offline**, which makes it a reliable option for planes, long car journeys and waiting rooms. It runs on iPhone and iPad.

## How to use any toddler app well

Even the best app works better with a few habits around it:

- **Play together when you can.** Naming the animals with your child and copying the sounds turns screen time into conversation time.
- **Keep sessions short.** Ten minutes of focused play beats an hour of drifting.
- **Use the end as a ritual.** A sticker, a song or a "bye-bye Bo" makes stopping easier.
- **Bring it into real life.** Spot the colours and animals from the app on a walk or in a picture book.

## FAQ

### What age is Baby Games for?

Babies and toddlers from 1 to 4 years old. There are three age levels, 1 to 2, 2 to 3 and 3 to 4, so the games match what your child can do.

### Does my child need to be able to read?

No. Bo talks your child through every game, and there is no reading, no timers and no way to lose.

### Are there ads in Baby Games?

No. There are no ads and no tracking, and purchases and the parent area are behind a grown-up check.

### Does Baby Games work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline, so it's ready for flights, car journeys and anywhere without wifi.

### How long should a toddler play?

Bo's daily Play Time lasts 5 to 10 minutes and ends with a sticker, which gives a natural, easy stopping point.
